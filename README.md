# 🚀 TASK 3 – AI Website Generation: InAmigos Foundation 🌐✨

Official website for **InAmigos Foundation (NGO)** created for **Task 3 (AI Website Generation)** of the internship program.

---

## 🌟 Overview

A modern, responsive, multi-section web platform built for **InAmigos Foundation** — a non-profit youth organization committed to child education, youth empowerment, environmental sustainability, and healthcare initiatives across India.

### 🎯 Key Sections Built

1. **Top Announcement Bar**:
   - Live banner highlighting active internship cohorts and quick applications.

2. **Header & Navigation**:
   - Brand logo with gradient icon, smooth section anchors, and mobile responsive menu.

3. **Homepage (Hero Section)**:
   - Empowering headline: *"Empowering Youth, Educating Children, Transforming Communities."*
   - Live social metrics card (50K+ Lives Impacted, 25K+ Children Educated, 10K+ Trees Planted, 2,500+ Interns Mentored).
   - Trust and accreditation badges.

4. **About Us Section (`#about`)**:
   - Organization history, mission statement, vision, and core operating values (Integrity, Transparency, Grassroots Impact).

5. **Services & Core Initiatives (`#initiatives`)**:
   - **Project Shiksha**: Stationery drives, free tutoring camps, and digital literacy.
   - **Green Earth & Afforestation**: Tree plantation and cleanliness drives.
   - **Youth Internship & Skill Hub**: Task-based practical learning for college students.
   - **Nutrition & Food Relief**: Ration distribution and mid-day meal support.
   - **Women Empowerment**: Menstrual hygiene kits and livelihood skills.
   - **Community Health**: Free medical screenings and medicine distribution.

6. **Internship & Student Hub (`#internships`)**:
   - Dedicated showcase highlighting the Task 1, Task 2, and Task 3 internship framework.
   - Step-by-step submission checklist.

7. **Interactive Donation & Pledge Calculator (`#donate`)**:
   - Interactive contribution selector (₹250, ₹500, ₹1000, ₹2500) calculating tangible impact (e.g., funding stationery for 2 children or planting 3 trees).

8. **Impact Voices & Testimonials (`#impact`)**:
   - Real reviews from past student interns, community beneficiaries, and environmental volunteers.

9. **Frequently Asked Questions (`#faq`)**:
   - Interactive accordion covering virtual internships, certification, donation transparency, and local volunteering.

10. **Contact & Volunteer Registration Form (`#contact`)**:
    - Interactive registration form with real-time submission confirmation state.
    - Official contact details, WhatsApp helplines, and pan-India headquarters.

11. **Footer**:
    - Quick navigation links, cause focus areas, social media handles, and copyright info.
